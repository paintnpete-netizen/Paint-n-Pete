## Brand 1 — Benjamin Moore

**Overview**
Benjamin Moore is a dealer-distributed brand with a deep color system and clear performance tiers — from production-grade Ultra Spec up through Aura. For most of our residential work, Regal Select is the everyday premium wall paint, Advance handles cabinets and trim that need an enamel look, and Element Guard earns its keep on Florida exteriors when the weather window is short.

### Lines we use

| Line | Where it belongs |
|---|---|
| **Aura Interior** | Appearance-critical walls; Bath & Spa for humid rooms rather than standard matte |
| **Regal Select Interior** | Whole-home walls, hallways, kitchens — eggshell/satin for washable rooms |
| **Scuff-X** | Stairwells, mudrooms, playrooms, rentals — walls that take abuse |
| **Advance** | Cabinets, doors, trim — furniture-like leveling; plan for the 16-hour recoat |
| **Aura Exterior** | Premium siding and trim, especially deep or color-critical exteriors |
| **Element Guard** | Humid elevations and rain-prone schedules — early rain resistance after 60 minutes |

### Pros (Florida)
- Element Guard has measurable early-rain guidance — useful in St. Pete summers.
- Aura Exterior is a strong choice for UV-exposed, color-critical work.
- Advance levels exceptionally well for cabinetry without solvent cleanup.
- Scuff-X is a real answer to mechanical wall damage, not just dirt.

### Cons (Florida)
- Aura sits at the top of the price ladder; Regal and Advance are still premium.
- Advance's slow recoat makes it vulnerable to dust and premature handling in humid houses.
- "Mildew resistant" on a can does not replace washing, drainage, or fixing the moisture source.
- Dealer inventory is less uniform than a company-store chain — plan ahead on specialty products.

**Best fit for us:** Regal Select eggshell for most occupied homes; Element Guard for rain-prone exteriors; Advance for carefully scheduled cabinetry.

## Brand 2 — Sherwin-Williams

**Overview**
Sherwin-Williams is the easiest brand to replenish mid-job — company stores everywhere, same SKU on the shelf. Emerald and Duration sit at the top; SuperPaint covers value work. For Florida exteriors, Latitude is the line with the clearest measurable weather-window data. For bathrooms, Duration Home is one of the better-documented moisture-oriented wall paints.

### Lines we use

| Line | Where it belongs |
|---|---|
| **Emerald Interior** | Premium whole-home walls where washability and appearance both matter |
| **Duration Home** | Bathrooms, kitchens, laundry — moisture-oriented interior |
| **Cashmere** | Living and dining rooms where a low-stipple, silky wall is the priority |
| **Emerald Urethane Trim Enamel** | Cabinets, doors, exterior doors — oil-like flow, water cleanup, 4-hour recoat |
| **Emerald Exterior** | Premium siding and trim |
| **Duration Exterior** | High-build protection on siding, stucco, masonry |
| **Latitude Exterior** | Hot substrates and short rain windows — ClimateFlex technology |

### Pros (Florida)
- Company-store network makes mid-job color matching and restocking reliable.
- Latitude publishes a 30-minute rain-avoidance window — useful, if not a license to paint onto damp surfaces.
- Duration Home is built for rooms that get cleaned often and see humidity.
- Emerald Urethane recoats faster than many waterborne alkyd cabinet enamels.

### Cons (Florida)
- Emerald is the top of the price ladder; promotions make list prices hard to compare.
- Emerald Exterior can set quickly above 80°F — lap marks are a real risk on sun-heated elevations.
- Sheen names and available bases vary by regional SKU — we specify the exact can, not a generic name.
- Older Resilience literature still floats online; we treat Latitude as the current weather-flexible exterior, not Resilience.

**Best fit for us:** Latitude for summer exteriors; Duration Home for bathrooms; Emerald Urethane for cabinetry and exterior doors.

## Brand 3 — Florida Paints

**Overview**
Florida Paints is manufactured in Florida for Florida. That claim is partly marketing — and partly backed by measurable PDS data on wind-driven rain, elongation, vapor permeance, and alkali resistance. Where it earns a place on our trucks is stucco and masonry work, coastal exteriors, and jobs where a local system and dealer support matter more than a national brand name.

### Lines we use

| Line | Where it belongs |
|---|---|
| **Legacy Interior / Exterior** | Premium whole-home interiors and conventional premium exteriors |
| **Seaside** | Coastal exteriors in flat or satin |
| **Glades** | Mid-tier exteriors when sheen flexibility and value both matter |
| **Encore** | Gloss trim, doors, and cabinets — especially over properly prepared aged alkyd |
| **SunFlex** | Premium stucco/CMU — published elongation, permeance, wind-driven-rain testing |
| **TropiFlex XL** | Masonry with recurring hairline cracking where a true elastomeric film is justified |
| **TropiCrete** | Pedestrian decks and pool decks after proper prep — not vehicular traffic |

### Pros (Florida)
- Technical data sheets publish real numbers: elongation, permeance, ASTM wind-driven-rain results.
- Product design directly addresses stucco, high alkalinity, vapor escape, and mildew pressure.
- Local manufacture and Florida-focused stores help with substrate-specific systems.
- Clear premium-to-value exterior tiers (Legacy → Seaside → Glades).

### Cons (Florida)
- Smaller distribution footprint — specialty products need planning.
- Most architectural lines are under 100 g/L VOC, not zero.
- Elastomeric/high-build coatings only work if film thickness is correct; thin application wastes the chemistry.
- "Florida-formulated" is a manufacturer claim, not an independent head-to-head win over the nationals. We say it that way.

**Best fit for us:** SunFlex for premium masonry; Legacy Exterior for conventional premium exteriors; TropiFlex only when an elastomeric system is actually warranted.

## Brand 4 — Farrow & Ball

**Overview**
Farrow & Ball is a color and sheen brand first. Exact published sheen percentages, unusually rich color appearance, and water-based chemistry throughout. It earns a place when the client wants that specific look — and only when we specify the right finish for the room. Estate Emulsion in a Florida bathroom is a mistake. Modern Emulsion is not.

### Lines we use

| Line | Where it belongs |
|---|---|
| **Modern Emulsion** (7% sheen) | Bathrooms, kitchens, humid rooms — washable, mould-protected |
| **Dead Flat** (2%) | Designer walls that need the ultra-flat look *and* scrub resistance |
| **Estate Emulsion** (2%) | Low-traffic dry rooms only — gently wipeable, **not washable** |
| **Modern Eggshell** (40%) | Hard-use cabinets, floors, stairs |
| **Estate Eggshell** (20%) | Interior trim and furniture with a softer sheen |
| **Exterior Masonry** | Compatible historic stucco/masonry — breathable; confirm substrate |
| **Exterior Eggshell / Full Gloss** | Exterior wood and metal within a suitable dry weather window |

### Pros (Florida)
- Exact sheen levels and color depth are the reason clients ask for it.
- Modern Emulsion and Dead Flat give practical alternatives to fragile Estate Emulsion.
- Water-based range, very low measured VOC, A+ indoor-air rating.
- Exterior products include UV and fungi/algae protection.

### Cons (Florida)
- Luxury pricing across the range; imported tin sizes and system primers add cost.
- Estate Emulsion fails in kitchens, bathrooms, entries, and kids' rooms — we will not specify it there.
- Advice sheets often require application below 80% RH — hard outdoors in a St. Pete summer.
- Ultra-matt, deeply pigmented walls can show localized touch-ups under side light.
- Limited local availability can slow repairs and reorders.

**Best fit for us:** Modern Emulsion for humid rooms; Dead Flat for durable designer walls; Estate Emulsion only in quiet dry rooms, and only when the client understands the tradeoff.

## How we choose

| Priority | Starting point |
|---|---|
| Premium conventional exterior | Benjamin Moore Aura Exterior or Sherwin-Williams Emerald Exterior |
| Short Florida rain window | Benjamin Moore Element Guard or Sherwin-Williams Latitude |
| Stucco / CMU with hairline movement | Florida Paints SunFlex (TropiFlex when true elastomeric build is needed) |
| Bathroom walls | SW Duration Home, F&B Modern Emulsion, or BM Bath & Spa / premium eggshell |
| High-traffic interior walls | BM Scuff-X, SW Emerald/Duration, F&B Dead Flat |
| Cabinets and trim | SW Emerald Urethane, BM Advance, F&B Modern Eggshell |
| Designer color and exact sheen | Farrow & Ball |
| Value whole-home repaint | BM ben / Ultra Spec, SW SuperPaint, Florida Paints Glades |

The product name is secondary to preparation. In coastal St. Petersburg we wash off salt and mildew, repair water entry, respect dew point, and avoid sun-heated surfaces. No premium coating compensates for painting contaminated, wet, or unstable material.
