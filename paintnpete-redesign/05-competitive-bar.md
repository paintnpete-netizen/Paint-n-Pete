# Competitive bar — Tampa Bay residential painters

Paint'n Pete must clear this bar to be equal or better than strong local competitors.
Full competitor canvas: tampa-bay-painting-competitors-july-2026.canvas.tsx

## Locals reviewed (July 2026)
- Attention to Detail — climate + warranty + pricing FAQ + estimate form
- Nu-Trend — awards, process, city blocks
- Five Star Pinellas — reviews volume, team, booking
- CertaPro St. Pete — multi-step estimate, neighborhood portfolio
- Highmark — before/after case studies, cost guide
- S&H Painting — process + before/after
- Premium Painters — speed promise + ratings + named team

## Must-have (local parity)
1. 10+ real pages — Interior, Exterior, Cabinets/Drywall, Work, About, Areas, FAQ, Contact/Estimate
2. Named process (consult → written proposal → prep → walkthrough)
3. Trust strip in first scroll: Google rating/count, licensed/insured, warranty (top independents claim 2–5 yrs)
4. Capioned local gallery + before/after (neighborhoods, scope)
5. Estimate form: ZIP, service type, optional photos, response-time promise + sticky phone
6. Mobile that doesn't horizontally scroll
7. About with Noah's story — locals win on personality
8. Service area list (St. Pete / Clearwater / Tampa / Pinellas) with local SEO H1s
9. Kill all generic Wix boilerplate (inquiry page “strategy session” copy)

## Beat the locals (differentiators)
1. Product education for BM / SW / Florida Paints / F&B with Florida-honest pros/cons
2. Monthly product review (editorial credibility)
3. Florida-specific exterior messaging (UV, humidity, salt, rain windows)
4. Portfolio-quality photography (already have from Noah Kanwal site)
5. Phase 2: AI Color Studio (no local competitor has this well)

## One-sprint MVP (clear the independent bar)
Trust + process homepage · three service pages · captioned Work gallery · real estimate form · Areas + FAQ · delete template copy. Franchise depth (city pages, booking) can follow.

## Do not copy
- Franchise zip-wall CTA spam
- Vague "premium luxury" with no process
- Uncaptioned photo dumps
