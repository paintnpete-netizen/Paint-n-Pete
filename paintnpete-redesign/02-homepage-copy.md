## 6. Why it lasts in Florida

**Section heading**
Why it lasts in Florida

**Body**
Florida is hard on paint. UV fades color, humidity slows cure times, salt air works at exteriors, and summer rain finds every gap in preparation.

So preparation is where the work actually happens. We wash and scrape down to sound substrate, repair rot and cracks before they spread, prime bare surfaces, and let coatings cure on their schedule instead of ours. We select products for the surface and the exposure, not for whatever is cheapest by the gallon.

That's the difference between a repaint that looks good on the final walkthrough and one that still looks good in five years.
