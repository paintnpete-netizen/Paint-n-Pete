About

A true
Craftsman.

Noah Kanwal is a residential painter based in St. Petersburg, Florida, with roughly fifteen years in the trade. He works at the scale of a house — full interiors and exteriors — with a craftsman’s patience for every surface.

Across more than three hundred completed projects, he has built a reputation for clean lines, durable finishes, and color chosen to suit the room it lives in. His work is made to hold up under hard Florida light and to reward a close look.

He works closely with the architects, designers, and homeowners who care about the last ten percent — the details most people never notice, and the ones he refuses to leave unfinished.

He is the owner of Paint’n Pete, a residential painting company based in St. Petersburg.
