# Operating Rhythm — Guide §9

A system you skip is worth nothing. These go in the calendar as recurring
events.

**Daily, 10 min.** Morning: check every inbound channel, reply to all of them
before starting work. Between jobs: post the day's pre-written caption, tag the
neighborhood. End of day: update the tracking sheet.

**Monday, 30 min.** Weekly content batch. Work the follow-up list — every
proposal at day 3, 8, or 21 gets its touch. Drop last week's photos into the
Canva before/after frame. Confirm automations ran and no lead fell through.

**First Monday monthly, 60 min.** Enter job-costing actuals and read the
variance. Review count check. Commercial pipeline review. Google Business
Profile refresh. Perplexity research on products and local market. Check the
past-client list for anyone hitting a repaint window.

## The only four numbers that matter early

Inquiries received · proposals sent · jobs won · reviews earned.

One row in a spreadsheet, thirty seconds a month. Everything else — followers,
impressions — is decoration until these are moving.

into each one, plus what to do when you fall off. The monthly scorecard sheet
