# Getting Found — Guide §4

**This is Phase 1 and the highest-return work in the entire system.** Twenty-one
Google reviews at 5.0, against 300+ completed projects, is the largest single
gap in the business and the cheapest to close. If 40% of the last 30 clients
respond, the public review count roughly doubles in a few weeks.

*(Read live 2026-08-19. The count was 14 when this project started, so reviews
are arriving on their own — which is a good sign for how the backlog will
respond when actually asked.)*

**Built:**
- `review-engine.md` — request texts and response templates
- `review-tracker.csv` — importable backlog tracker with pacing
- `SETUP.md` — the click-by-click Google-account steps
- `gbp-content-pack.md` — description, categories, services, 8 Q&A, 4 posts
- `content-engine.md` — weekly batch prompt, hashtag bank, photo list for crew
- `web-templates.md` — service-area and case study page specs

no review link, no review responses, no posts, no Q&A, no services. See

Also needed: the last 30 completed clients with contact details, and

page beats eight copies.
