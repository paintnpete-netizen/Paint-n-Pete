## 4. Eight questions for the Q&A section

Post these yourself from a second Google account, then answer them from the
business account. This is a supported use of the feature and it fills the space
before a competitor or a confused customer does.

**Q1. Do you paint occupied homes, or do we need to move out?**
Occupied homes are most of our work. We agree the plan with you before anything
comes off the truck, protect floors and furniture, seal the work zone from the
rest of the house, and restore the room at the end of each day.

**Q2. How long does an exterior repaint take in Florida weather?**
It depends on the size of the house and the condition of the surfaces, but
weather is the real variable. Coatings have temperature, humidity, and recoat
windows that we respect rather than work around. We would rather add a day than
trap moisture under a coat.

**Q3. What kind of prep do you do on an exterior?**
Wash, full dry-out, scrape and sand all failing coating to a sound edge, repair
rot, kill mildew rather than paint over it, caulk the joints that move while
leaving drainage gaps open, and prime every bare or repaired area. Prep is the
majority of the labour on a proper exterior.

**Q4. What paint brands do you use?**
Benjamin Moore, Sherwin-Williams, Florida Paints, and Farrow & Ball. The product
is chosen for the surface and its exposure, not for price per gallon.

**Q5. Do you paint kitchen cabinets?**
Yes. Cabinets are degreased, sanded, primed with an adhesion primer, and sprayed
rather than brushed, which is what gives the finish its factory-grade surface.

**Q6. What areas do you serve?**
St. Petersburg and the surrounding Pinellas and Hillsborough communities,
including the Old Northeast, Snell Isle, Kenwood, Gulfport, Tierra Verde, and
St. Pete Beach.

**Q7. Do you give estimates over the phone?**
No. An accurate number requires seeing the surfaces, because the condition of
what's underneath drives the labour more than the square footage does. We do a
walkthrough and send a written proposal.

## 6. Photo rules

- Upload real job photos only. Stock imagery is obvious and it undercuts the one
  advantage a small local company has.
- Geotagging is not required and Google has said it doesn't use EXIF location.
  Don't waste time on it.
- Add photos steadily rather than dumping thirty at once.
- Before/after pairs must be shot from the same angle or they read as dishonest.
- Get permission before publishing any photo of a client's home.

## 7. Maintenance

Fifteen minutes, once a week, in the Monday routine:

1. Respond to any new review (templates in `review-engine.md`).
2. Publish one post from the rotation.
3. Add one or two photos from last week's work.
4. Check for and reject any "suggested edits" Google or a user has made to your
   hours, category, or address.
