## The brief

Paint'n Pete is a residential and light-commercial painting company based in
St. Petersburg, Florida, owned and operated by Noah Kanwal. Kanwal has worked in
the trade for approximately fifteen years and has completed more than three
hundred projects across Pinellas and Hillsborough counties.

The company offers five services: interior painting, exterior painting, cabinets
and fine finishes, drywall and surface repair, and light commercial work. It
specifies coatings from Benjamin Moore, Sherwin-Williams, Florida Paints, and
Farrow & Ball, selecting products according to the substrate and its exposure
rather than the price per gallon. In Florida this distinction is practical
rather than philosophical: salt air, sustained direct sun, and year-round
humidity will find any coating chosen for cost alone, usually within two
seasons.

The company's working position is that preparation constitutes most of the job.
Washing, scraping, sanding, repair, priming, and masking determine how a finish
looks after five years; the topcoat determines how it looks on the final day.
Coatings are allowed to cure on their own schedule rather than the schedule the
calendar would prefer, and Paint'n Pete will extend a timeline rather than
compress a cure.

Typical clients are homeowners, interior designers, and architects working on
properties where the finish is expected to hold up under close inspection —
clients who care about the last ten percent. The company works in occupied homes
and coordinates with designers and general contractors.
