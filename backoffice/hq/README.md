# Back Office HQ

This is the office. One page. Bookmark it.

It is **not** the customer website. Do not deploy it to paintnpete.com, and do
not submit it to Google.

## Open it

In Cursor: open `backoffice/hq/index.html` and use **Open in Browser**, or from
Terminal:

```bash
open /Users/noahkanwal/Projects/paint-n-pete/backoffice/hq/index.html
```

Then star it on the Chrome bookmark bar. That is the front door.

Optional: Chrome → Settings → On startup → Open a specific page → this file.

## What it is

A hallway of rooms that open the live tools: the Back Office Google Sheet,
Gmail, KaiCalls, Calendar, GBP, and the templates in this folder. Today’s
clipboard sits on the way in (inbox sweep, post, close the day). Mondays and
first Mondays get an extra block automatically.

Copy buttons on the front desk are the replies you send from a ladder.

## Calendar

Use the **Add event** buttons in the Rhythm room once. Save each as repeating.
Full checklists stay in `../08-routines/operating-rhythm.md`.

## Google Sites

You do not need a Google Site. This HTML *is* the HQ, and Sites cannot import
it cleanly. If you later want `hq.paintnpete.com` behind a login, that is a
separate (paid or identity) host — do not put it on the public Netlify site.

## Second operator

Swap the sheet URL, mailbox, and Kai dashboard after they fill
`../config/business-profile.yml`. Credentials never copy. Until then this HQ
is Paint'n Pete’s office, not a generic kit.
