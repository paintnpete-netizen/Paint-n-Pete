Homeowner Q

“Can you just match the color?”

People ask if we can match what’s already on the wall. Sometimes yes. Often the existing film has yellowed, chalked, or been topped with a different sheen, so a perfect match on the can still looks wrong in the room. Better approach: confirm the color in your light, on your wall, before we open the first gallon. That’s what we did on this [PROJECT TYPE] in [NEIGHBORHOOD].

Mistake to avoid

Paint over chalky exterior

If you drag a hand across sun-facing stucco or wood and it comes back dusty, that’s chalk. New paint on chalk peels. Wash and a bonding primer first — then the finish coat. We see this a lot on Pinellas exteriors that were “touched up” between full jobs.
