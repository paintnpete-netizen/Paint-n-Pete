# Lead Capture, Response & Follow-Up — Guide §3

The contractor who answers first wins a disproportionate share of the work.
Standard: a real human reply within five minutes during working hours, by 8am
otherwise.

**To build:** four standard responses (form inquiry, missed-call text-back,
ballpark-price question, out-of-area decline), three-touch follow-up sequence
(day 3 / 8 / 21), objection response library, 12-question consultation intake
form spec.

**Depends on:** Business Brief. The intake form also needs the leads sheet from
`07-automation/` to land responses somewhere.

**Rule:** every response ends with a scheduling question, never "let me know."
