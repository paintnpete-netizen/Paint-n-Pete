# Hiring & Onboarding

The whole point of the SOPs is that they make hiring survivable. You are not
looking for someone who already paints the way you paint — you are looking for
someone who will follow a written standard and tell you the truth when something
goes wrong.

---

## Job ad

> Post to Indeed, Facebook local groups, and the supply house counter. Fill in
> the bracketed items from `config/business-profile.yml` before posting.

**Residential Painter — [City], FL**

Paint'n Pete is a small residential painting company working in [service area].
We do interior and exterior repaints for homeowners who care how the work holds
up, not just how it looks on the last day.

We are looking for a painter who can run a room without being watched.

**What the work is:** interior repaints, exterior repaints, cabinet and trim
work. Brush, roll, and spray. Prep is the majority of the job and we treat it
that way.

**What we need from you:**
- Reliable transportation and a phone you answer.
- You show up at the agreed time or you call before that time.
- You can follow a written procedure and say so when you can't.
- Comfortable on a ladder and around homeowners.
- [X] years of production painting, or enough skill to show us in a working
  interview.

**What we offer:**
- [$X–$X per hour, DOE] paid [weekly/biweekly].
- Steady work, not day-labour churn.
- Materials and quality tools supplied.
- Written procedures so you know what "done" means before you start.
- [Paid time off / holidays / other benefits — or delete this line]

**To apply:** text [phone] with your name, years of experience, and one photo of
work you're proud of.

*Paint'n Pete is an equal opportunity employer.*

> **Before posting:** confirm the pay range, benefits, and worker classification
> with your accountant. If you intend to hire subcontractors rather than
> employees, this ad is written wrong — talk to your accountant first, because
> misclassification is expensive and the fines are personal.

## Ten screening questions

Ask these by phone, in this order. You are listening for specifics. Vague
answers to questions 3, 5, and 9 are the ones that predict problems.

1. Walk me through the last exterior repaint you worked on — what was the prep?
2. How do you decide when a washed exterior is dry enough to coat?
3. Tell me about a job where something went wrong. What did you do?
4. What's your process for protecting an occupied home's floors and furniture?
5. A homeowner tells you the colour looks wrong after two walls. What happens
   next?
6. What tools do you own?
7. How do you handle a wall that looks fine until you hit it with raking light?
8. What time do you normally start, and what happens when you're running late?
9. Have you ever had to tell a boss you made a mistake that cost money? How did
   that go?
10. What do you want out of your next job that you're not getting now?

**Then a paid working interview.** One day, one room, on a real job, at your
hourly rate. You will learn more in that day than in five interviews. Watch
whether they set up before they paint, and whether they tell you about a mistake
or hide it.

## First-week onboarding

**Day 0 — before they start**
- Paperwork: W-4, I-9, direct deposit, emergency contact. Confirm with your
  accountant that this is complete for your state.
- Add to the crew group chat and the shared calendar.
- Confirm they've read the three SOPs. Give printed copies.

**Day 1**
- Ride along or work alongside. Do not send them out alone.
- Walk the interior protection SOP on a live job, step by step.
- Explain the one non-negotiable: *if something goes wrong, you tell me the same
  day. Nobody has ever been fired here for reporting a mistake.*

**Day 2–4**
- They run setup unsupervised; you inspect it.
- Introduce the exterior prep SOP on a live exterior.
- Show them how the punch list and final walkthrough work, even if they're not
  running it yet.

**Day 5**
- Sit down for fifteen minutes. Two questions: what's unclear, and what's
  slowing you down?
- Correct one thing. Only one. Pick the one that costs the most money.

**Week 2 onward**
- They own a room, then a job, then a walkthrough.
- Every correction goes back into the SOP so the next hire never has to learn it
  from a mistake.

## Franchise note

An operator who copies this system inherits the SOPs, the ad, and the questions.
What they must supply themselves is the pay range, the local labour market
reality, and legal review of employment classification in their state. Those are
flagged `todo` in `config/business-profile.yml` and must not be inherited
blindly.
