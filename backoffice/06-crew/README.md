# Crew, SOPs & Hiring — Guide §6

The reputation rests on prep standards that currently live in Noah's head.
Growth — and franchising in particular — means those standards have to exist
outside of him.

**Built:**
- `sop-interior-protection.md` — setup and protection standard, EN + ES
- `sop-exterior-prep.md` — prep standard, EN + ES
- `sop-final-walkthrough.md` — punch list and closeout, EN + ES
- `hiring.md` — job ad, ten screening questions, first-week onboarding

**Spanish versions are drafts, not finals.** AI translation is a strong first
draft and a poor final one, especially for trade terminology. Every translation
in this folder is marked as needing review by a fluent speaker before it goes in
the van. That review is an open item.

**Open items before the SOPs are usable as-written:** confirm the pay range and
worker classification in `hiring.md` with your accountant, and confirm the
warranty wording in the walkthrough SOP matches the proposal template.

**Note for the franchise layer:** these SOPs are the most transferable asset in
the whole system. A new operator inherits fifteen years of standards on day one.

**Rule of thumb:** a callback is usually a missing SOP. Add one per quarter as
gaps appear.
