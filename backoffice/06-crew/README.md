# Crew, SOPs & Hiring — Guide §6

The reputation rests on prep standards that currently live in Noah's head.
Growth — and franchising in particular — means those standards have to exist
outside of him.

**To build first, in this order:** Interior Protection & Setup, Exterior Prep
Standard, Final Walkthrough Checklist. One page each, numbered, with the quality
standard for each step rather than just the action.

**Then:** job ad, ten phone-screen questions, first-week onboarding plan.

**Spanish versions are required, not optional.** AI translation is a strong first
draft and a poor final one, especially for trade terminology. Every translation
gets reviewed by a fluent speaker before it goes in the van.

**Note for the franchise layer:** these SOPs are the most transferable asset in
the whole system. A new operator inherits fifteen years of standards on day one.

**Rule of thumb:** a callback is usually a missing SOP. Add one per quarter as
gaps appear.
