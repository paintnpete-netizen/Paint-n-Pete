# SOP — Exterior Prep Standard

One page. Print it, laminate it, keep it in the van.

> **The standard:** prep determines how this looks in five years. The topcoat
> only determines how it looks on the last day. If we are behind schedule, we do
> not take it out of prep.

Florida-specific: salt air, sustained UV, and humidity will find every shortcut.
The failure point is almost always a joint, an edge, or a coating applied over
something that wasn't sound.

---

## Checklist

**1. Walk the elevation and mark defects before washing.** Chalk or tape every
soft spot, split, failed caulk joint, and area of peeling. *Done correctly:* the
homeowner sees the marks and knows what we found before we start.

**2. Wash. Then let it dry properly.** Pressure appropriate to the substrate —
lower on wood and stucco than most crews use. *Done correctly:* no water driven
behind siding, no fuzzed wood grain, and a genuine dry-out period before coating.

**3. Re-inspect after washing.** Washing exposes rot and failed coatings that
weren't visible before. *Done correctly:* anything newly found is photographed
and reported before it gets covered — see the change-order process.

**4. Scrape and sand all failing coating to a sound edge.** Feather the
transition. *Done correctly:* run a hand over it — you should not feel the step
between old coating and bare substrate.

**5. Treat and repair.** Wood rot cut out and replaced or filled per its extent;
mildew killed, not painted over. *Done correctly:* no coating goes over anything
soft, damp, or organic.

**6. Caulk the joints that move.** Trim-to-siding, window and door perimeters,
penetrations. Not horizontal drainage gaps — those must stay open. *Done
correctly:* joints are sealed, drainage still works.

**7. Prime every bare or repaired area**, matched to the substrate. Spot-prime
before full coats. *Done correctly:* nothing bare goes under a topcoat.

**8. Mask and protect** windows, roofing, hardscape, plants, and the neighbour's
car. *Done correctly:* overspray reaches nothing we didn't intend.

**9. Check conditions before coating** — temperature, humidity, dew point, and
what the sky is doing in three hours. *Done correctly:* we do not start a coat
we cannot finish and let set.

**10. Coat to the specified film build**, not to the point where it looks
covered. Respect recoat windows.

---

## Common mistakes

Coating too soon after washing, which traps moisture. Pressure-washing hard
enough to drive water behind siding. Painting over mildew. Caulking the
horizontal gaps that are supposed to drain. Feathering that leaves a visible
ridge under the topcoat. Starting the sun-facing elevation in the afternoon
heat. Treating the recoat window as advisory.

# PROCEDIMIENTO — Preparación de Exteriores

> **BORRADOR — requiere revisión de un hablante nativo antes de usarse.**

**El estándar:** la preparación determina cómo se verá esto en cinco años. La
capa final solo determina cómo se ve el último día. Si vamos atrasados, no se
recorta la preparación.

**1. Recorra la fachada y marque los defectos antes de lavar:** zonas blandas,
grietas, sellador fallado y pintura descascarada.

**2. Lave. Luego deje secar bien.** Presión adecuada al material — menor en
madera y estuco de lo que se acostumbra. No fuerce agua detrás del revestimiento.

**3. Vuelva a inspeccionar después de lavar.** El lavado revela pudrición y
recubrimientos fallados. Fotografíe y reporte todo hallazgo nuevo antes de
cubrirlo.

**4. Raspe y lije toda pintura suelta hasta borde firme.** Difumine la
transición: al pasar la mano no debe sentirse el escalón.

**5. Trate y repare.** Madera podrida: cortar y reemplazar o rellenar según el
alcance. El moho se elimina, no se pinta encima.

**6. Selle las juntas que se mueven:** molduras contra revestimiento, perímetros
de ventanas y puertas, penetraciones. **No** selle las ranuras horizontales de
drenaje.

**7. Imprima toda área desnuda o reparada** con el imprimante adecuado al
material.

**8. Enmascare y proteja** ventanas, techo, pavimentos, plantas y vehículos
vecinos.

**9. Verifique las condiciones antes de aplicar:** temperatura, humedad, punto de
rocío y el pronóstico de las próximas tres horas.

**10. Aplique el espesor especificado**, no hasta que "se vea cubierto". Respete
los tiempos de recubrimiento.

**Errores comunes:** aplicar muy pronto después del lavado; lavar con demasiada
presión; pintar sobre moho; sellar las ranuras de drenaje; difuminado que deja
un borde visible; empezar la fachada soleada en la tarde.
