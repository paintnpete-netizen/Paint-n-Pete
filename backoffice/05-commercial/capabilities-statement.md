brokers, either as the first attachment or the thing you leave behind after a
site visit. It is not a brochure — it exists so a decision-maker can qualify you
in ninety seconds without asking you three rounds of questions.

### Where we're different

**Occupied-space work.** Most of our residential experience is in homes people
are living in, which is the same discipline an occupied office, clinic, or
tenanted building needs: sealed work zones, protected floors and furnishings,
dust control at the HVAC returns, and the space restored at the end of every
shift rather than at the end of the job.

**After-hours and phased scheduling.** Work is sequenced around your tenants and
operations, not around our convenience.

**Coastal exposure specification.** Products are specified for substrate and

claim in three years.

**A written prep standard.** Our crews work from documented procedures for
interior protection, exterior prep, and final walkthrough. The standard does not
change based on who is on site that day.

## The ready folder

Create a Drive folder named **`Compliance — send on request`** (owned by
`noah@paintnpete.com`, not the consulting Gmail) containing:

1. Current certificate of insurance (PDF)
2. W-9, signed and dated (PDF) — see below
3. Florida business registration / local business tax receipt (PDF) — not a contractor license
4. This capabilities statement (PDF)
5. Reference sheet — three projects with contactable references

### How to file the W-9

Do this once. The PDF stays in that Drive folder. It does not go in git, email
drafts, or this repo.

1. Download IRS Form W-9 from [irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/fw9.pdf](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/fw9.pdf).
2. **Line 1 (Name):** `Kanwal Consulting LLC` — the legal name, not Paint'n Pete.
3. **Line 2 (Business name / DBA):** `Paint'n Pete`.
4. **Tax classification:** LLC. Check the LLC box and enter the tax
   classification letter that matches how the LLC actually files (C, S, or
   disregarded / sole prop). If you are unsure, that letter is on last year's
   return or from whoever filed the LLC — do not guess.
5. **Address:** 1105 18th St S, St. Petersburg, FL 33712.
6. **TIN:** the LLC's EIN. If the LLC is disregarded and you have been using
   an SSN, stop and confirm with whoever does your taxes before you write
   either number on this form.
7. Sign, date, export as PDF named `Kanwal-Consulting-LLC-W9.pdf`.
8. Put it in the Drive folder. Signed copy is on file as of 2024-12-09;
   `credentials.w9_on_file` is verified. Keep the send PDF in Drive, not git.

When a GC asks, attach that PDF and the current COI in the same reply, same
hour. Do not type the EIN into chat, git, or the website.

Set a calendar reminder for thirty days before each COI expires. WC expires
2027-01-13 (remind 2026-12-14). Next GL expires 2027-07-15 (remind
2027-06-15). An expired certificate on file is how a sub quietly stops
getting invited to bid.

The current Next PDF is issued to a specific additional insured. When a
different GC asks, request a new certificate in their name — do not reuse
that holder.

**Why this matters:** when a GC's admin asks for your COI and W-9, they are
usually assembling a bid package under a deadline. The sub who sends both within
the hour gets included. The sub who says "let me call my agent" often doesn't.
