# Capabilities Statement

Two pages. It goes to general contractors, property managers, and commercial
brokers, either as the first attachment or the thing you leave behind after a
site visit. It is not a brochure — it exists so a decision-maker can qualify you
in ninety seconds without asking you three rounds of questions.

---

## ⛔ Publishing gate

Every `[BRACKETED]` item below is `todo` or `unverified` in
`config/business-profile.yml`. **Do not send this document to anyone until they
are filled with verified values.** A capabilities statement with the insurance
limits missing is worse than no capabilities statement — it tells a GC's admin
you aren't ready to be on a jobsite, and you rarely get a second look.

The blocking items are: `credentials.license`, `credentials.insurance`,
`credentials.warranty`, `credentials.w9_on_file`, `company.legal_name`,
`company.founded`, and `contact.email` (a free Gmail address on a capabilities
statement reads as a one-man operation regardless of whether you are one).

### Where we're different

**Occupied-space work.** Most of our residential experience is in homes people
are living in, which is the same discipline an occupied office, clinic, or
tenanted building needs: sealed work zones, protected floors and furnishings,
dust control at the HVAC returns, and the space restored at the end of every
shift rather than at the end of the job.

**After-hours and phased scheduling.** Work is sequenced around your tenants and
operations, not around our convenience.

**Coastal exposure specification.** Products are specified for substrate and
exposure. On the Gulf coast, a coating chosen on price per gallon is a warranty
claim in three years.

**A written prep standard.** Our crews work from documented procedures for
interior protection, exterior prep, and final walkthrough. The standard does not
change based on who is on site that day.

## The ready folder

Create a Drive folder named **`Compliance — send on request`** containing:

1. Current certificate of insurance (PDF)
2. W-9, signed and dated (PDF)
3. Contractor licence documentation (PDF)
4. This capabilities statement (PDF)
5. Reference sheet — three projects with contactable references

Set a calendar reminder for thirty days before the COI expires. An expired
certificate on file is how a sub quietly stops getting invited to bid.

**Why this matters:** when a GC's admin asks for your COI and W-9, they are
usually assembling a bid package under a deadline. The sub who sends both within
the hour gets included. The sub who says "let me call my agent" often doesn't.

## Design

Build it in Canva. Two pages, one accent colour, generous white space, your logo
top-left. Export as PDF named `PaintnPete-Capabilities-[YYYY-MM].pdf` so the
recipient can tell at a glance whether they're looking at a current version.

Do not use stock photos of people in hard hats. One or two real project photos
from `../../images/work/` if you use any at all.
