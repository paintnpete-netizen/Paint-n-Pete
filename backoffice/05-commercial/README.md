# Commercial Growth — Guide §5

Residential revenue depends on how many homeowners find you this month.
Commercial revenue depends on relationships built once and harvested for years.

**To build:** two-page capabilities statement, GC/builder introduction email,
designer and architect outreach, property manager outreach, bid package
analyzer prompt.

**Depends on:** licensing, insurance limits, and warranty terms — all currently
`todo` in config. **This section is blocked until those are verified.** A
capabilities statement without insurance limits is what disqualifies you.

**Also needs:** a ready folder holding live W-9, certificate of insurance,
license, and a one-page reference sheet. Being able to send these within an hour
separates you from most small subs.

**The differentiator to lead with:** occupied-space and after-hours capability,
plus a high-end residential finish standard applied to commercial work.
